# E2 under spec v0.3 — Credulity sweep re-run (θ from 0 to 0.7)

Segment G4c, 2026-09-30. Spec v0.3 (NOTEBOOK D10: grown nodes get a τ exit back to their anchor). Spec §10 E2, §5.2, §5.4, §8, §14. Defaults: q_edge 0.08, θ 0.4 (D7, D8). The v0.2 baseline is `results/E2.md` (tag `v0.2-results`) and is left unchanged.

## 1. Plain summary

Each simulated mind checks whether a partner is really responding to it, and θ sets how strict that check is. After the fix that stops minds getting stuck on newly grown parts of their world, minds develop much further: at the default setting, about 7 in 10 reach the second level, compared with about 2 in 10 before. How often a tape recording fools a mind barely changed. At the default of 0.4 it is now about 1 in 22,000 encounters, and even the pessimistic end of the estimate (1 in 7,700) meets the project's "less than 1 in 5,000" rule. Stricter settings still slow early development without improving where minds end up, and past 0.5 development stalls, just as before. The recommendation is to keep θ at 0.4.

## 2. Method

The E2 design is unchanged: the E0 population (20 minds plus 4 objects, 4 recordings and 4 mirrors, Erdős–Rényi p = 0.2), 300 epochs, and all other parameters at their defaults (`experiments/E2.yaml`). Measures, statistics (exact Clopper–Pearson for rates, normal 95% intervals over seeds for development) and the θ = 0 diagnostic are defined as in `results/E2.md` §2.

**Grid (cut to fit ~30 min on 2 workers on a shared machine).** v0.2 used 208 runs. This re-run uses 66 runs, with the seeds placed where the decision is made:

| θ | 0 | 0.2 | 0.3 | 0.35 | **0.4** | 0.45 | 0.5 | 0.6 | 0.7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| seeds | 4 | 4 | 6 | 8 | **24** | 8 | 4 | 4 | 4 |

θ = 0.1, 0.25 and 0.8 were dropped. v0.2 showed that 0.1 and 0.25 sit on a smooth part of the curve and that 0.8 is dead. The diagnostic uses 2 seeds instead of 6. Seeds are 0..n−1 at every θ. Wall time was about 28 min in total (θ = 0.4: 13 min; the rest: 14 min), with the machine at a load of about 30.

**Commands** (from the repo root):

```
uv run python -m experiments.E2 run --design v03        # sweeps (outputs/E2v03_d40, _d35, _coarse, _d30) + diagnostic (outputs/E2v03_diag.json)
uv run python -m experiments.E2 run --design v03 --skip-existing   # what was actually run for the remainder, after θ = 0.4 finished
uv run python -m experiments.E2 analyze --design v03    # tables, outputs/E2v03_summary.json, results/E2_v03_*.png
```

`--design v03` is new in `experiments/E2.py`. The v0.2 defaults (`--design v02`) are unchanged. The v0.3 run was split in two because the first launch still had a larger grid, which was cut down once it was clear the machine was contended. The θ = 0.4 sweep comes from the first launch and everything else from the second. Both use the same code and seeds.

- **Final level ≥2 is flat at 0.68–0.73 for 0.2 ≤ θ ≤ 0.5.** θ = 0 is higher at 0.85 ± 0.10, but with only 4 seeds, and it also has the most false recognition. θ = 0 is the one point where fooled minds may be contributing to development.
- **Speed still falls with θ.** Mean level at epoch 25 is about 1.45 for θ 0.2–0.35, then 1.27 at 0.4 (about −13%), 0.98 at 0.45 and 0.68 at 0.5. Unlike v0.2, speed keeps rising below 0.2 (1.88 at θ = 0).

Under v0.3 the median C of a mind-partner would-be recognition is **0.43** (5–95%: 0.18–0.67). For a recording it is **0.07** (95th percentile 0.20). The v0.2 level-0 median was about 0.15, and only 3% of level-0 mind windows survived θ = 0.4 (now 50%). Minds now look far more contingent to each other, while recordings look the same. The likely reason is that partners are no longer parked on absorbing grown nodes with 1–3 labels, so they answer the offers made to them. θ = 0.4 now sits between the recording tail and the middle of the mind distribution, not in the thin mind tail.

## 5. Recommendation on θ

**Keep θ = 0.4.** It is still the lowest grid θ whose 95% upper bound meets the D8 recording criterion (1.3e-4 < 2e-4), now with more margin. It costs about 13% of early speed against θ 0.2–0.35 and nothing detectable in final development (0.71 against 0.68–0.73). θ = 0.35 is not safe to adopt: its point estimate sits on the line and its upper bound is 2.5× over. θ = 0.45 gives no improvement in final development and costs another ~23% of early speed; its false-positive rate is below measurement (0 / 21k), so it would only be worth it if the criterion were tightened. v0.3 also widens the gap between how contingent minds and recordings look, which makes 0.4 more robust than it was under v0.2.

## 6. Caveats

- **Fewer seeds than v0.2** away from θ = 0.4 (4–8 per point). Development CIs at those points are ±0.06–0.11, and the recording rates at 0.3–0.35 rest on 4–12 counts. The θ = 0.4 criterion check rests on 3 counts in 67k windows.
- **θ = 0 level ≥3 = 0.50 ± 0.00** is 4 seeds that happened to agree, not a real zero-variance result.
- The v0.2 comparison uses the numbers in `results/E2.md`, since the v0.2 raw outputs were not kept. The v0.2 comparison curve at 0.5–0.8 splits the pooled 101,628 zero-count windows evenly, which only affects where its ▽ markers sit.
- 300 epochs. At θ ≥ 0.45, development is still rising at epoch 300, so final values there are lower bounds.
- The diagnostic uses 2 seeds (232 recording windows, only 23 with a level ≥1 mind), so its recording tail is coarse. It explains the shape of the results but does not replace the sweep counts.
- In the trade-off figure, the labels for θ = 0.35, 0.45 and 0.5 overlap in the left panel. `E2_v03_by_theta.png` and `E2_v03_vs_v02.png` show the same data clearly.
- The machine was heavily loaded (four other experiment agents plus a 6-worker control run). This affects wall time only, not results (the runs are seeded and deterministic).
