# E2 — Credulity sweep (θ from 0 to 0.8)

Segment G4b, 2026-09-29. Spec §10 E2, §5.2, §5.4, §8, §14. Current defaults: q_edge 0.08, θ 0.4 (NOTEBOOK D7, D8).

## 1. Plain summary

Each simulated "mind" decides whether a partner is really responding to it by checking how well the partner's answers match what it offered. The strictness of that check is a single dial, θ. We turned the dial from fully lenient (0) to very strict (0.8) and watched two things: how often minds were fooled by a tape-recording of another mind, and how far minds developed. At the lenient end, minds were fooled in about 1 of every 16 encounters with a recording and developed no further than at moderate settings; they only got there sooner. Past 0.5 the check became so strict that minds stopped recognizing each other and development stalled. The current setting of 0.4 is the most lenient on our grid that meets the project's "fooled less than 1 time in 5,000" rule. The price is slower early development (roughly 1 in 7 fewer levels gained early on), with little change in how far minds eventually get.

## 2. Method

**Population.** The E0 population: 20 minds plus 4 objects, 4 recordings and 4 mirrors on one Erdős–Rényi contact graph (p = 0.2). All other parameters are at their defaults. The only change from E0 is **300 epochs instead of 500**, to fit the compute budget (`experiments/E2.yaml`).

**Grid.** θ ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.25, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8} with 10 seeds each (`E2_coarse`). θ ∈ {0.3, 0.35, 0.4, 0.45} with 32 seeds each (`E2_dense`), because the recording false-positive rate crosses the 1-in-5,000 criterion there and rates near 1e-4 need ~10⁵ windows. That makes 208 runs, run through `experiments/sweep.py` with 3 workers and `--keep-runs` (27 min wall, 79 CPU-min). Seeds 0–9 of the dense grid use the same seed numbers as the coarse grid.

**Measures, per θ (runs pooled):**
- **False-positive rate per control kind** = one-sided recognitions of that kind ÷ mind–control windows of that kind, summed over all epochs and seeds (§5.4, §10). The 95% intervals are **exact Clopper–Pearson**. For zero counts, the upper bound is quoted.
- **Development:** the fraction of minds at level ≥1, ≥2 and ≥3, and the mean level, at the final epoch (300). Speed: the mean level at epoch 25, and the first epoch at which the seed-averaged mean level reaches 1. Intervals are normal 95% intervals over seeds.
- **Contingency computable:** the fraction of mind windows with |O| ≥ 15 and ≥ 2 labels, as a per-epoch fraction averaged over all epochs, then over seeds. This is the same convention as C0.

**Diagnostic** (`experiments/E2_diag.py`, 6 seeds × 300 epochs at θ = 0). At θ = 0, every window that passes the structural part of §5.3 (a joint cycle, or a probe plus an extra) and has computable C ≥ 0 counts as a one-sided recognition. For each such window the diagnostic logs C, the partner kind, and the mind's level. The share of those windows with C ≥ θ shows how the threshold filters minds versus recordings, before any feedback from θ on development. It wraps `sim.runner.run_episode` at runtime inside its own process, and no shared code is edited.

**Commands** (from the repo root):

```
uv run python -m experiments.E2 all       # both sweeps + diagnostic + tables + figures (~28 min, 3 workers)
uv run python -m experiments.E2 analyze   # re-analyze outputs/E2_coarse, outputs/E2_dense, outputs/E2_diag.json
```

The raw data is in `outputs/E2_coarse/`, `outputs/E2_dense/` (sweep.json plus per-run dirs), `outputs/E2_summary.json` and `outputs/E2_diag.json`.

- **Final development is flat for θ ≤ 0.45.** Level ≥1 stays at 0.81–0.84 and mean level at 1.28–1.51. Level ≥2 is 0.28 pooled over θ ≤ 0.35, against 0.23 at 0.4 and 0.20 at 0.45. That is a modest dip, with overlapping seed intervals at 0.4.

### 3.3 Why: the C distribution of would-be recognitions (diagnostic, θ = 0 runs)

This is the share of structurally qualifying, computable windows with C ≥ θ:

| θ | mind partner, all (n = 6,436) | mind, mind at level 0 | mind, level ≥1 | recording partner, all (n = 1,207) | recording, level 0 | recording, level ≥1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 | 0.36 | 0.25 | 0.91 | 0.071 | 0.062 | 0.23 |
| 0.3 | 0.21 | 0.082 | 0.80 | 0.0091 | 0.0053 | 0.076 |
| 0.4 | 0.13 | 0.031 | 0.62 | 0.0008 | 0 | 0.015 |
| 0.5 | 0.084 | 0.012 | 0.43 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 0.6 | 0.042 | 0.0038 | 0.23 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 0.7 | 0.017 | 0.0006 | 0.097 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

At level 0, a mind's partner looks only mildly contingent: median C is about 0.15, because of ρ = 0.5 plus the uniform fallback. The 0→1 step therefore relies on the thin upper tail. A level-0 would-be recognition of a mind survives θ = 0.4 only 3% of the time, but there are so many opportunities that level ≥1 at epoch 300 is unchanged. At 0.6 the survival is 0.4% and must happen in **both** agents in the **same** window (mutuality), so the 0→1 step collapses. Probing minds (level ≥1) produce much more contingent windows (62% survive at 0.4), which is why the higher levels are less θ-sensitive. Recordings are separated from minds by one to two orders of magnitude at every θ ≥ 0.2. The residual false positives at θ = 0.4 come from windows where a level ≥1 mind probes the recording, which agrees with E0-diag.

## 4. Interpretation against the spec

- **§14 "Lowering θ raises development speed and false recognition together (E2)": partly supported.** Lowering θ from 0.5 to about 0.35 raises both, as predicted: early speed rises about 2.7× and the recording rate rises from < 3.6e-5 to 3.3e-4. Below 0.35, lowering θ **only** raises false recognition, by 180× from 0.35 to 0. Speed and the final plateau are both saturated there. The trade-off is therefore not a smooth frontier. It is an L-shape (see `E2_tradeoff.png`), with the knee at θ ≈ 0.35–0.4.
- **Operating point.** θ = 0.4 sits just past the knee, on the safe side. It is the lowest grid θ that meets the §11/D8 recording criterion, including its 95% upper bound, and costs about −14% in early speed and a modest, statistically marginal dip in level ≥2 (0.23 vs 0.28). It is well clear of the stall region (≥ 0.55). **θ = 0.4 is a reasonable operating point.** Its criterion margin is thin (upper bound 1.8e-4 vs a limit of 2e-4). θ = 0.45 would buy a ~7× lower rate at the cost of about 2× slower early development and level ≥2 of 0.20.
- **Controls:** objects and mirrors are excluded structurally at every θ, including θ = 0. Recordings are the only control whose exclusion depends on θ, which confirms the reasoning in D8.
- **Phase-transition-like behavior in θ:** the stall is sharp. Level ≥1 falls from 0.77 to 0.29 to 0.01 across θ = 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, and mutuality (squaring a small tail probability) is the likely amplifier. This is relevant to E3.

## 5. Surprises and caveats

- **Development plateaus early and low regardless of θ.** At θ ≤ 0.35 the level ≥2 fraction stops changing by epoch ~40, at ~28%, with most minds stuck at level 1. So the binding constraint on development is not contingency. Candidates are the equal-level, same-window mutual rule and the low probe success noted in D7. This makes "stalled development" in E2 mostly a question of **speed**, not final state, for θ ≤ 0.5.
- **θ = 0 is not "no check".** C ≥ 0 plus computability still applies, and the joint-cycle structure still excludes objects and mirrors. That is why the recording rate at θ = 0 is 6%, not higher.
- **300 epochs, not 500.** The false-positive rate depends on the mix of mind levels, since level ≥1 minds are fooled more often. The agreement with E0 at θ = 0.3 and 0.4 suggests the difference is small.
- **Seed CIs on development are wide** with 10 seeds (±0.06–0.09 on level ≥2). The level ≥2 dip at 0.4 and 0.45 relative to low θ is suggestive, not established. The θ = 0.35 bump (0.31) is within noise.
- The diagnostic runs at θ = 0, where minds develop faster, so its level mix differs from runs at higher θ. It has only 66 level ≥1 recording windows, so its recording tail is coarse. It explains the shape of the results but does not replace the sweep counts.
- "Computable" is an unweighted mean of per-epoch fractions (the C0 convention). sweep.json keeps only the final epoch, so per-run metrics.csv files were needed.

## 6. Suggested follow-ups

1. **Significance-based contingency test** (rejected for now in D8). The mind and recording C distributions overlap mostly at level 0 with small |O|. A test that scales with |O|, such as a one-sided binomial or permutation p-value, may beat any fixed θ on both axes. E2's harness can evaluate it directly.
2. **Diagnose the early plateau** (with E3/E4): why does development stop at ~28% level ≥2 by epoch 40? Log how often a mutual same-level pair qualifies one-sidedly but not jointly.
3. If the D8 criterion margin matters, use **θ = 0.45**, or run ~100 seeds at θ = 0.4 with 500 epochs to tighten the estimate (E0-final already gives 8.2e-5 [5.8, 11.4]e-5, which is comfortable).
4. Repeat the sweep at ρ = 0.3 and 0.7. The mind-side C distribution scales with ρ, so the stall θ should move with it while the recording curve should not.
